Diversity of post-training data is **critical** for LLM performance, but existing **text-based metrics** provide **weak signals** for task-relevant features. We introduce **Feature Activation Coverage (FAC)** to measure data diversity in an **interpretable feature space**, and propose **FAC Synthesis**, a diversity-driven data synthesis framework. We use a **sparse autoencoder** to identify **missing task-relevant features** from a seed dataset and generate synthetic samples. Our approach **consistently improves** data diversity and downstream performance across various tasks: **instruction following**, **toxicity detection**, **reward modeling**, and **behavior steering**. We identify a **shared, interpretable feature space** across model families (LLaMA, Mistral, Qwen), enabling cross-model knowledge transfer. This work provides a practical methodology for exploring **data-centric optimization** of LLMs.
