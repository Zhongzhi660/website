**In summary:**

1. We theoretically derive an **upper bound** on post-training generalization error, emphasizing **task-relevant feature coverage**.

2. We introduce **FAC** as a **model-aware diversity metric** defined in the model's internal feature space.

3. We propose **FAC Synthesis**, a framework that **identifies missing task-relevant features** and **generates targeted synthetic samples**. Experiments on **four tasks** and **three open-source model families** show that FAC Synthesis outperforms baseline methods.
